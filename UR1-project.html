Lane Follower Robot

This project was completed as a requirement of the Unified Robotics I course at Lawrence Technological University. Using standard chassis platforms, students are tasked with designing, ordering and assembling a microcontroller board capable of controlling two DC motors, as well as implementing basic computer vision techniques to detect lane markings and respond accordingly. Final report and demonstration of the robotic system was collected at the end of the semester.

As the first requirement of the project, we began by designing our microcontroller boards. We learned the basic functions of EagleCAD, and were provided a library of components that would be needed to successfully implement the microchip, motor controllers, LCD screen, and other features of the setup. This was, by far, the most enjoyable and rewarding portion of the entire project. While it was challenging in the beginning, the feeling of recieving the microcontroller board, soldering the components, and successfully testing the chip for the first time is unmatched.
